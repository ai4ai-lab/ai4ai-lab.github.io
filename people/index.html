members of the AI4AI Lab

s.parbhoo@imperial.ac.uk

Sonali Parbhoo

Assistant Professor, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College London. Lab lead.

I received my PhD (summa cum laude) in 2019 from the University of Basel, Switzerland, where I built intelligent models for understanding the interplay between host and virus in the fight against HIV. I was previously a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University and a Swiss National Science Fellow. I was named a Rising Star in AI in 2021.

My research develops trustworthy machine learning methods for high-stakes decision-making, particularly in healthcare, spanning sequential decision-making under uncertainty, causal inference, and interpretable machine learning.

dominic.marshall12@imperial.ac.uk

Dominic Marshall

Dominic is a Clinical Research Fellow and PhD candidate at Imperial College London, and a Specialist Registrar in Respiratory and Intensive Care Medicine. His PhD, funded by a Medical Research Council Clinical Research Training Fellowship, builds models to identify phenotypes of acute lung injury from multimodal longitudinal data, with a focus on acute hypoxic respiratory failure and ARDS. He originally trained in molecular and cellular biology at the University of Bath.

r.majumdar24@imperial.ac.uk

Ritam Majumdar

Ritam is a third-year PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Imperial College London, supervised by Sonali Parbhoo. His research is in reinforcement learning, with a focus on off-policy evaluation, inverse reinforcement learning, theoretical guarantees and interpretable RL. Before joining Imperial in April 2024, he graduated from the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati in 2021 with a major in Chemical Engineering and a minor in Mathematics. From August 2021 he was a researcher at the Tata Research Development and Design Centre (TRDDC) in Pune, India, developing foundation models for partial differential equations.

nikita.narayanan18@imperial.ac.uk

Nikita Narayanan

Nikita is a third-year PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Imperial College London, supervised by Sonali Parbhoo. Her research focuses on continual learning for foundation models and concept-based representations for healthcare. She leads primary and secondary school STEM sessions as part of Imperial’s outreach programme, helping students reinforce skills in maths and physics as well as inspiring them to see how these apply in the real world.

elizaveta.sheremetyeva@imperial.ac.uk

Elizaveta Sheremetyeva

Elizaveta is a PhD student in the AI4Health Centre for Doctoral Training at Imperial College London. Her project, Developing an Artificial Intelligence agent to optimise decision-making during assisted conception, builds reinforcement learning methods for personalised IVF treatment. She is supervised by Thomas Heinis, Waljit Dhillo, Ali Abbara and Sonali Parbhoo. She is interested in how AI can help people make better decisions in the real world, starting with healthcare. She has co-led Women in Computing at Imperial and previously represented England in international chess.

haoyang.hong22@imperial.ac.uk

Haoyang Hong

Haoyang is a PhD student at Imperial College London, supervised by Sonali Parbhoo. His research focuses on causal representation learning and robust machine learning for healthcare, particularly out-of-distribution generalisation and causal approaches to improving model robustness. His research explores both the theory and practical effectiveness of these methods, and the development of reliable machine learning models. Haoyang also has experience in clinical predictive modelling, reinforcement learning and large language models, including medical multi-agent systems, with applications in healthcare and clinical decision-making.

yuxin.wang1@lmu.de

Yuxin Wang

Yuxin is a PhD student in Computer Science at LMU Munich, in the Institute of AI in Management supervised by Stefan Feuerriegel, and is affiliated with the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML) and relAI. She is visiting the AI4AI Lab. Her research covers semiparametric and debiased machine learning for treatment effects, causal survival analysis under informative censoring, and causal inference for LLM alignment, including off-policy evaluation and policy learning from preference and observational data, with applications in medicine and economics.

pranav.sharma25@imperial.ac.uk

Pranav Sharma

Pranav is a second-year Electrical and Information Engineering undergraduate at Imperial College London and a Laidlaw Scholar, conducting research under Dr Sonali Parbhoo. His research currently focuses on advancing Concept Bottleneck Models to develop more interpretable and reliable machine learning models for healthcare.

Beyond his research, Pranav helped lead ArterialAge in developing a wearable device for earlier detection of heart failure, becoming UK champions and placing among the global top 10 out of 687 teams at the 2026 Silicon Labs IoT Challenge.

His broader interests lie in translating advances in machine learning into technologies that can make a meaningful real-world impact.
