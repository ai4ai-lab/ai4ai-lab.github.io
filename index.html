AI4AI Lab

AI for Actionable Impact

Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College London

The AI4AI Lab (AI for Actionable Impact) is a machine learning research group at Imperial College London. We build AI that people can act on: models whose outputs can be used to make real decisions in high-stakes settings.

Research

Our logo shows the three cornerstones of our work, causality (C), interpretability (I) and uncertainty (U), each informing the others, with people at the centre. We work on three central questions:

  • People · I How can humans and AI work together for consequential decisions? (learning to defer, concept-based models, inverse reinforcement learning)
  • U How do we interact with the environment, while avoiding risk but learning as quickly as possible? (meta-learning, Bayesian optimisation, model selection, uncertainty quantification)
  • C How should AI act when its decisions change the world it learns from? (off-policy evaluation, distribution shift, continual learning, causal discovery, causal inference and experimental design)

These questions are relevant for small-data decision-making (e.g. clinical decision systems) and for large-scale foundation models.

Join us: we are looking for PhD students interested in reinforcement learning, causal inference, Bayesian methods and interpretability with applications to healthcare. Contact sp.ai4ai.lab@gmail.com.
