Sonali gave an invited talk, *Dharma, Demonstration and the Alignment of Machines*, at [Hinduism and AI: A Roundtable](https://deploymentof.ai/events/hinduismAI2026) at the University of Cambridge, a full-day meeting on what Hindu thought can contribute to the design, use and governance of intelligent systems. The roundtable was convened by the Stanford Law AI Initiative, Cambridge's Centre for Human-Inspired Artificial Intelligence and the BAPS Swaminarayan Research Institute.
