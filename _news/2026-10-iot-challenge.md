A student team supervised by Sonali reached the global top 10 of the Silicon Labs IoT Challenge 2026, the highest-placed UK team, with ArterialAge, a wearable prototype for earlier detection of heart failure. The team pitched in the final in Hanoi. Congratulations to [Pranav Sharma](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pranav-sharma11/), [Abhi Kapoor](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abhi-kapoor-806924281/), [Krish Thakrar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/krishthakrar-engineering/), [Anjali Ketan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anjali-ketan-847280247/) and [Joshika Manikandan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joshika-manikandan-150092231/).
