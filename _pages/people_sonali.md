## Sonali Parbhoo

Assistant Professor, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College London. Lab lead.

I received my PhD (summa cum laude) in 2019 from the University of Basel, Switzerland, where I built intelligent models for understanding the interplay between host and virus in the fight against HIV. I was previously a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University and a Swiss National Science Fellow. I was named a **Rising Star in AI in 2021**.

My research develops trustworthy machine learning methods for high-stakes decision-making, particularly in healthcare, spanning sequential decision-making under uncertainty, causal inference, and interpretable machine learning.
