## Sonali Parbhoo

Sonali Parbhoo is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Imperial College London, where she leads the AI4AI Lab. Previously, she was a Postdoctoral Fellow at Harvard University, supported by a Swiss National Science Foundation fellowship. She received her PhD (summa cum laude) from the University of Basel in 2019, where her work pioneered AI-driven treatment optimisation for HIV. Her research develops explainable, causal and probabilistic methods for high-stakes decisions made by clinicians and AI systems from imperfect evidence. Sonali has contributed methods for assessing decisions under uncertainty before deployment, for evaluating decision policies when outcomes are sparse, and inverse methods for examining the objectives behind the behaviour of foundation models. She has published more than 60 papers in machine learning venues such as ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, AISTATS and KDD, and in medical and scientific journals including Nature Communications, The Lancet Digital Health and Intensive Care Medicine. She was named a Rising Star in AI in 2021 and received a CIFAR award in 2024.
