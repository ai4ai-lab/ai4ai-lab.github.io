## <a class="people-name-link" href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=FwEz5s4AAAAJ" title="Google Scholar">Sonali Parbhoo</a>

Sonali Parbhoo is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Imperial College London, where she leads the AI for Actionable Impact (AI4AI) Lab. Previously, she was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences at Harvard University, supported by a Swiss National Science Foundation fellowship. She received her PhD (summa cum laude) from the University of Basel, and her MSc from the University of the Witwatersrand, South Africa. Sonali has published more than 60 papers in machine learning venues such as ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, AISTATS, KDD and JMLR, and in medical and scientific journals including Nature Medicine, The Lancet Digital Health, JAMA and Intensive Care Medicine. She has co-organised Machine Learning for Healthcare (2025, 2026) and Advances in Approximate Bayesian Inference (2024, 2025), as well as workshops at ICLR, NeurIPS and ICML, and serves as an Area Chair for ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, RLC and MLHC. Her honours include a Rising Star in AI Award (EECS, Harvard, MIT 2021) and a CIFAR ELLIS Award. Her vision is to create actionable AI and ML systems that work with humans to improve scientific inference and predictive capabilities in healthcare, focused on challenges of reliability and robustness.
