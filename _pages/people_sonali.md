## Sonali Parbhoo

Sonali Parbhoo is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at Imperial College London, where she leads the AI for Actionable Impact (AI4AI) Lab. Previously, she was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the John A. Paulson School of Engineering and Applied Sciences at Harvard University, supported by a Swiss National Science Foundation fellowship. She received her PhD (summa cum laude) from the University of Basel, and her MSc and BSc from the University of the Witwatersrand, South Africa. Her research studies how humans and AI can make better decisions together in high-stakes settings: how people and AI should share consequential decisions, how to tell whether those decisions are reliable, and what happens when AI changes the systems it operates in. She develops causal, probabilistic and interpretable methods for sequential decision-making and evaluation, and leads efforts to deploy machine learning models for risk prediction and early failure detection in critical care at Hammersmith Hospital.

Sonali has published more than 60 papers in machine learning venues such as ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, AISTATS, KDD and JMLR, and in medical and scientific journals including Nature Communications, The Lancet Digital Health, JAMA and Intensive Care Medicine. She has co-organised Machine Learning for Healthcare (2025, 2026) and Advances in Approximate Bayesian Inference (2024, 2025), as well as workshops at ICLR, NeurIPS and ICML, and serves as an Area Chair for ICML, NeurIPS, ICLR, RLC and MLHC. Her honours include a Rising Star in AI (2021), a CIFAR ELLIS Catalyst Award, best paper awards at AMIA (2017, 2021) and the NeurIPS Machine Learning for Health workshop (2016), and the University of Basel Faculty of Science Best Thesis Award.
