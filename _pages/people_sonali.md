## Sonali Parbhoo

Assistant Professor, Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, Imperial College London. Lab lead.

Sonali leads the AI4AI Lab, which bridges machine learning and clinical decision-making. The lab builds explainable, causal and probabilistic methods that support high-stakes decisions made by clinicians and AI systems from imperfect evidence.

She received her PhD (summa cum laude) from the University of Basel in 2019, where her work pioneered AI-driven treatment optimisation for HIV by modelling the interplay between host and virus. She was then a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University, supported by a Swiss National Science Foundation fellowship. At Imperial, her group has developed methods for assessing decisions under uncertainty before deployment, for evaluating decision policies when outcomes are sparse, and inverse methods for examining the objectives behind the behaviour of foundation models.

Her critical-care research has appeared in Nature Communications, The Lancet Digital Health and Intensive Care Medicine. She has authored more than 60 papers. Her honours include being named a Rising Star in AI (2022) and a CIFAR award (2024).
