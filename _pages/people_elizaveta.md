## Elizaveta Sheremetyeva

PhD student

Elizaveta is a PhD student in the AI4Health Centre for Doctoral Training at Imperial College London, co-supervised by Sonali Parbhoo and Ali Abbara. She builds reinforcement learning methods for personalised IVF treatment, and is interested in how AI can help people make better decisions in the real world, starting with healthcare. She has co-led [Women in Computing](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing/about/equality-and-diversity/women-in-computing/elizaveta-sheremetyeva/) at Imperial and previously represented England in international chess.
