## <a class="people-name-link" href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=jO76VAQAAAAJ" title="Google Scholar">Elizaveta Sheremetyeva</a>

Elizaveta is a PhD student in the AI4Health Centre for Doctoral Training at Imperial College London. Her project, *Developing an Artificial Intelligence agent to optimise decision-making during assisted conception*, builds reinforcement learning methods for personalised IVF treatment. She is supervised by [Thomas Heinis](https://wp.doc.ic.ac.uk/theinis/), [Waljit Dhillo](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/w.dhillo), [Ali Abbara](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/ali.abbara) and [Sonali Parbhoo](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/s.parbhoo). She is interested in how AI can help people make better decisions in the real world, starting with healthcare. She has co-led [Women in Computing](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/computing/about/equality-and-diversity/women-in-computing/elizaveta-sheremetyeva/) at Imperial and previously represented England in international chess.
