## <a class="people-name-link" href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/pranav-sharma11/" title="LinkedIn">Pranav Sharma</a>

Pranav is a second-year Electrical and Information Engineering undergraduate at Imperial College London and a Laidlaw Scholar, conducting research under Dr Sonali Parbhoo. His research currently focuses on advancing Concept Bottleneck Models to develop more interpretable and reliable machine learning models for healthcare.

Beyond his research, Pranav helped lead ArterialAge in developing a wearable device for earlier detection of heart failure, becoming UK champions and placing among the global top 10 out of 687 teams at the 2026 Silicon Labs IoT Challenge.

His broader interests lie in translating advances in machine learning into technologies that can make a meaningful real-world impact.
